Portal Page Design Plan

Student: Isaac

1. Purpose

This portal page will be the hub for all my WDD 130 work. The style is anime inspired with a strong mission board theme. It will be simple enough to reuse later as a portfolio.

2. Logo

My logo files are saved in the images folder:

  • images/logo-main.png
  • images/logo-horizontal.png
  • images/logo-icon.png

The design uses the initials IM with a katana and colorful brushstroke aura.

3. Color Scheme

  • Crimson Red: #D62839
  • Ember Orange: #FF6A2E
  • Electric Blue: #0D6CFF
  • Void Purple: #4B2EFF
  • Charcoal Black: #0B0E11
  • Steel Silver: #C7CBD1

4. Font Choices

  • Headings: Bebas Neue (Google Fonts)
  • Body: Inter or Roboto (Google Fonts)
  • Optional accent: Caveat Brush

5. Page Sections

  • Header: logo and page title
  • Nav: Home, WWR, ICE, About Me
  • Main: intro, list, table, form
  • Footer: name and class info

6. Required HTML Elements

  • Headings: h1 h2 h3
  • Paragraphs: intro and descriptions
  • Links: menu and assignment links
  • List: panel of assignments
  • Table: assignment summary
  • Form: small contact form
  • Image: logo and other images
